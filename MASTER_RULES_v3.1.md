**## 3. ACCURACY RULES AND PAID/UNPAID CLASSIFICATION**

**## 3.1 Paid Website Business Types**

\- Trader

**## 3.2 Unpaid / Rejected / Non-Paid Categories**

\- Indian (IN) and Chinese (CN) websites are not for work, subject to the Hong Kong and Macau country-menu exceptions described later.

\- Armor/weapons manufacturing, trading, supply, exporting, repairing services, or websites involving weapons imagery are strictly denied.

\- Porn/adult content websites are not acceptable.

\- Online gambling / online game websites are not acceptable.

\- General service-provider websites such as restaurants, salons, retailers, etc. are not acceptable, except Industrial Service Providers.

\- Food, drinks, food manufacturing, edible products, cold drinks, snacks, food imagery, bottle imagery, smoking imagery, alcohol-bar imagery, tobacco-consumption imagery are not acceptable.

\- Drug-making / pharmaceutical drug-making company websites are not acceptable; medical-equipment / medical- machinery companies are considerable.

\- Hacking service-provider / hacking-information websites are not acceptable.

\- Alcoholic/tobacco product manufacturers, suppliers or traders are not acceptable; related product or consumption imagery is also not acceptable.

\- Airline / aviation industry websites are not acceptable.

\- Personal websites, blogs, and similar personal/informational websites are not acceptable.

\- Websites hosted on free platforms such as WordPress are not acceptable.

\- Subdomain websites not hosted at root level cannot be accepted.

\- Animal trading, live or dead, is not accepted.

\- Websites in non-English language without a translation option are not accepted.

\- URL-redirecting websites are non-considerable; the supplied website should remain in its actual form.

**## 3.3 Accuracy Threshold**

\- All data is subject to checking.

\- Data can be rejected on a daily basis when accuracy falls below 80%.

\- Delivery partner must maintain minimum 80% accuracy daily.

\- Mandatory fields must be filled correctly. Failure to fill information exactly as required or as it appears on the site is counted as an error.

**## 4. END-TO-END WORKING FLOW**

1\. Receive the exact supplied URL.

2\. Open the site and determine website status without relying on a browser that automatically translates non-English content.

3\. Check for restricted/non-paid categories immediately. If the site clearly falls in a non-paid/restricted category, select the appropriate option and submit without wasting time on unnecessary fields.

4\. Confirm whether the site is root-level or a disallowed subdomain / double-domain / redirecting URL.

5\. Identify the company/business from About, Profile, History, Who We Are, Company Overview, or equivalent company-information tabs.

6\. Determine the main/leading/worldwide business type; apply Manufacturer priority when multiple business types are present and permitted.

7\. Validate country against Country Name List.xlsx, including Correct/Incorrect/Not Working handling and Correct Name mapping.

8\. For paid type, collect actual email, phone/mobile, full address, city, and state/region. Prefer the first matching set of contact details and keep them from the same place where possible.

9\. Verify at least 3 distinct qualifying products, with corresponding product names, proper descriptions, and qualifying copyable images.

10\. Apply all image rules: accepted formats only; no PDF, geometric, diagram, dummy/foggy restrictions as applicable; image must relate to the product name and description.

11\. Recheck every mandatory field against the portal error rules and source website.

12\. Make the final call: all mandatory checks pass = QUALIFIES; any failed mandatory requirement = SKIP.

13\. Return actual verified details only. Never fill a missing detail with an assumption or placeholder.

**## 6. BUSINESS TYPE DECISION LOGIC**

\- Manufacturer: preferred when clearly stated as the main/leading business and otherwise allowed by the rules.

\- Industrial Services: only when services support large-scale manufacturing industry; examples include metal polishing, powder coating, fabrication, refurbishment, welding, cutting and moulding.

\- Not Industrial Service: corporate services, software services, consultation services, small-scale industry services and similar general services.

\- Trader / Exporter / Wholesaler / Distributor / Supplier: only accepted when the business type is clearly established from the permitted company-information sources.

\- If the website is not one of the allowed paid business types, select the relevant non-paid/respective option and submit without investing time in the full paid fields.

\- A business type taken only from a homepage heading or generic heading is not accepted unless the special homepage-heading condition from the guideline is satisfied.

**## 7. INDUSTRIAL SERVICES EXCEPTION**

\- Industrial Service Providers can be considered using different qualifying services with descriptions and images, with at least 3 services.

\- The business-type option remains Industrial Services.

\- Industrial services are helping services for large-scale manufacturing industry.

\- Examples: metal polishing, powder coating, fabrication, refurbishment, welding, cutting, mouldings.

\- Corporate services, software services, consultation services, and small-scale industries are not treated as Industrial Service.

**## 8. ONLINE SELLING / PAYMENT GATEWAY RULE**

\- Online payment gateway, Add to Cart, Buy option or similar online selling can be considered for Manufacturer only, provided all other conditions are fulfilled.

\- Supplier, Distributor, Wholesaler, Trader and Exporter websites with online selling are not considered paid under this rule; select Online Shopping as instructed.

\- If a supplier has no qualifying product available, select Supplier, set product count to 0, and submit it as non-paid.

**## 10. CONTACT DETAIL RULES**

\- Email must be the exact actual email shown on the source website; no masked/placeholder/fabricated email.

\- If a valid email cannot be found/verified, do not invent one. Under the user's strict working rule, missing mandatory detail means SKIP.

\- Phone must be a real non-toll-free/non-fax phone/mobile number. Toll-free numbers are skipped.

\- Phone output must contain numbers only: remove +, -, parentheses, spaces, dots, slashes and other special characters.

\- Example: +44 (0) 1405 746000 -> 4401405746000.

\- Address, city, state and contact details should be taken from the same place where possible; use the first matching set to minimize recheck.

\- Facebook may be used for address/email/number when the source guideline permits it; if the Facebook page also has the number, use the number too.

**## 11. PRODUCT VALIDATION SYSTEM**

\- Minimum count is 3 distinct products.

\- The three products must be real qualifying products and not just services, projects, brands, programs, labels, signs, categories, or generic business offerings.

\- For every counted product, there should be a corresponding product name, proper product description, and qualifying copyable image.

\- Minimum 3 qualifying product images are required.

\- Allowed image file formats: JPEG, PNG, JPG, GIF.

\- PDF images, geometrical images, diagram images do not count.

\- Image must be related to the named product. Do not count unrelated stock images, generic scenery, people, animals, aircraft, etc. as product proof.

\- Descriptions must establish the nature/relevance of the product; table descriptions may count.

\- If fewer than 3 qualifying products, descriptions, or images are actually verified, the record fails the requirement and is SKIP/rejection as applicable.
